# SSRE2 Review Dimensions

Apply only the assigned operator to the sealed Scope. Cite registered identities and evidence. Send observation
requests to the Scope owner. Use the [Review method](method.md#3-produce-and-calibrate-five-audits) for result
shape and calibration. The controller owns cross-Dimension relations.

## Sonnet

- State each material quantity, method, or state relation with expected behavior, normalization, and regime.
- Trace each handoff's owner, conditions, and preserved and changed meaning.
- Locate false equivalence across regimes, precision, representation, determinism, failure, or returned state.
  Also locate divergence where a relation should hold.

A finding names its **CLM/COR**, structural locus, and observable prediction failure.

## Simple

- Locate owners of scientific objects, conversions, method and regime choice, state, invariants, and evidence.
- Trace selection through computation to returned state. Identify what complexity would return to callers if the
  Module were removed.
- Locate duplicated formulas, thresholds, conversions, selectors, or state rules with scientific consequence.

A finding cites the complete duplicate-owner or shallow-Interface chain.

## Reliable

- Recover unit, alias, normalization, tolerance, method-selection, and regime contracts.
- Trace legal and invalid transitions, interruption, retry, and re-entry. Locate where invariants are established,
  checked, and exposed. After interruption, inspect persistent and returned state.
- Seek apparent success despite a scale or coordinate mismatch, out-of-regime method, invalid-state reuse,
  non-finite value, or stale output. Request the cheapest check for an unresolved edge or transition. Inject
  faults only at a real scientific Seam.

For **FAIL**, locate a triggerable path inside the regime the target presents as supported.

## Evidenced

For every material **CLM/COR**:

- Identify the strongest analytic result, authoritative datum, independent implementation, or measurement.
- Challenge the claim or correspondence under declared normalization and regime through dimensions, agreement,
  limits, convergence, conservation, transforms, round trips, or complementary outputs.
- Trace circularity through shared formulas, data, expected relations, tolerances, and conversions.
- Give each remaining **UNK** the cheapest contradictory acquisition or targeted check and its independence limit.

Use the Review method's Evidenced calibration for counterexamples and evidence-specific contradictions.

## Evolvable

- Identify observed variation in unit or scale, method, regime, representation, backend, precision, state, or
  evidence.
- Trace one material change through Interfaces, owners, selectors, transitions, returned states, tests, and
  evidence. Report the actual modification set and where propagation stops.
- Locate shadow selectors, duplicate state machines, and wrappers or registries without real responsibility.
  Apply the Kernel's Adapter Seam rule.

Assess bounded, explainable change in the observed trace.
